# Auditable Flourishing v6.2 Master Reference Catalog

**Status:** Version-matched master catalog for the AF v6.2 protocol family.

This catalog is the single release-level bibliography. Individual companions contain only the works they directly cite. Inclusion here means that a source is cited somewhere in the version-matched family or is retained as a verified contextual source; it does not mean that every companion relies on every entry. Current factual sources remain date-bounded to the release evidence snapshot of 12 August 2026.

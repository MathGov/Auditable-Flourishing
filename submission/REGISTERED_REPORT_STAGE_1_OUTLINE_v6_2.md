# AF v6.2 Registered Report - Stage 1 Outline

## Research object
An independently governed exploratory Phase 0 evaluating AF v6.2 administration on frozen synthetic cases.

## Before submission
Constitute the materials-ready gate: frozen twenty-case library, hashes, practice bank, reviewer qualification record, immutable deposit system, analysis scripts and capacity plan.

## Primary exploratory outcomes
- criterion-specific and final-status reliability with justified intervals;
- audit-theatre discrimination;
- perturbation proposal, admission, rejection and destabilization rates;
- burden, abandonment and access failures;
- protocol refusals and administration defects.

## Independence
Separate sponsor, host, scenario custodian, reviewers, adjudicator, statistician, data custodian and publication authority. No author, sponsor, candidate or host veto over null/adverse publication.

## Boundary
In-principle acceptance can reduce publication bias; it does not itself establish operational independence, affected-party legitimacy, legal conformity, safety or authorization.

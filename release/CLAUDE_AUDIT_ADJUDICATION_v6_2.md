# AF v6.2 Independent Audit Adjudication

Only findings that reproduced against the complete official package, or that produced a scientifically stronger and more inspectable boundary, were implemented.

## Findings already closed in v6.0 and regression-tested in v6.2

- Active-version and artifact-reference synchronization.
- One canonical AF-SB12 definition across the Core and Methods Article.
- One explicit Stage A precedence across the Core, Anchors, Methods Article, workbook, and reference logic.
- DOCX/Markdown/PDF synchronization of floor tiers and correction closure.
- Corrected worked-perturbation title and result.
- Refreshed companion navigation and Measurement Handbook numbering.
- Perturbation-sensitivity provenance.
- ISO-8601 illustrative timestamps.
- Joint Stage A error, anti-shield/adoption incentive, and prospective burden clarifications.
- Phase 0A/0B sequencing while retaining Stage B as a provisional specification.

## New findings accepted for v6.2

- The v6.0 provenance folder contained two byte-identical audit files, one with a misleading final-synchronization filename.
- Artifact authority was inferable but not exposed in one canonical machine record.
- Current empirical anchors should have a separate dated verification record rather than remain buried within manuscript references.
- The root verifier should detect active release drift and duplicate provenance without depending on a spreadsheet library.
- A final deposit package benefits from a single `START_HERE.md` route.

## Findings not implemented as stated

- Stage B was not removed. Its empirical programme may be sequenced after Stage A feasibility, but the comparison architecture remains a defining component.
- Marginal criterion coefficients were not multiplied as if errors were independent. Joint Stage A performance must be estimated directly under observed or explicitly simulated dependence.
- Stable component identifiers were not mechanically renamed to v6.2. `AF-SB12-v5.6` and `AF-SB-RELATION-v6.0` remain because their controlling semantics did not change.
- Internal artifact verification was not relabelled as empirical validation, certification, due diligence, or deployment authority.

## Final disposition

AF-v6.2 is specification-complete, publication-integrity verified, and Phase 0 design-ready. The architecture is frozen for independent empirical contact unless new evidence establishes a genuine defect.

# AF v6.2 Known Limitations

- No external inter-reviewer reliability evidence has yet been established.
- No independent constitutional convention has established plural legitimacy.
- The frozen calibrated Phase 0 scenario library, qualified reviewer pool, and independent host are not yet constituted.
- Stage B remains a provisional comparison specification and may be empirically sequenced after Stage A feasibility.
- AF does not infer joint Stage A reliability by multiplying marginal criterion coefficients; joint false-clearance and false-exclusion behavior remains an empirical question.
- The anti-shield boundary reduces some private incentives to volunteer for review; candidate recruitment and non-participation bias remain programme risks.
- The contemporary fact-verification record is date-bound and must be refreshed when claims or sources change.
- Internal artifact verification does not establish construct validity, causal benefit, architecture fairness, burden acceptability, or downstream outcomes.
- No AF finding constitutes legal compliance, moral approval, community consent, certification, due diligence, foreseeability, procurement approval, or deployment authorization.
